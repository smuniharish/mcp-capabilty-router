# Community standards

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Discuss ideas and code rather than
people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and publishing private information
without permission are not acceptable.

These standards apply in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project
spaces. Report concerns privately through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community,
and will consider reports fairly and confidentially.
